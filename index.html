OUR BEGINNING · 01

救下一只动物，不是故事的结尾。

Saving an animalis only the beginning.

亚天宠物乐园成立于2016年，持续开展流浪动物救助与照护，为它们提供食物、安全住所、基础医疗、日常照护和活动空间。真正的救助从带它离开危险开始，还包括治疗、康复、日复一日的照顾，以及在它准备好以后，认真寻找不会再次放弃它的人。

Founded in 2016, Yatian Pet Paradise has continuously rescued and cared for homeless animals, providing food, safe shelter, basic medical care, daily support and space to play. True rescue begins with removing an animal from danger. It continues through treatment, recovery and daily care, followed by finding a family that will never abandon them again.

基地日常Life at theShelter

基地的每一天，都由许多细小而重要的工作组成。

Every day at the shelter is made up of many small but important acts of care.

01

饮食与清洁Food and clean spaces

按时准备食物和清洁饮水，打扫并消毒生活区域。

We prepare food and fresh water on schedule, and clean and disinfect living areas.

02

健康观察Health checks

留意每只动物的身体状况和精神状态，及时发现异常。

We watch every animal's physical condition and mood so changes can be noticed early.

03

活动与陪伴Play and companionship

狗狗每天分批到安全区域奔跑、晒太阳和玩耍。

Dogs take turns visiting a safe area each morning and afternoon to run, play and enjoy the sun.

冬天，我们尽力提供温暖舒适的休息空间；夏天则做好通风和降温，确保饮水干净充足。这些日复一日的照顾，是为了让它们安全、健康地生活，也为将来进入新的家庭做好准备。

In winter we provide warm, comfortable resting spaces. In summer we focus on ventilation, cooling and plentiful clean water. This daily care helps animals live safely and prepares them for future homes.

一段完整的救助旅程A complete rescue journey

从被发现，到重新拥有安心的生活

From being found to livingsafely again

每一步都需要时间、耐心，也需要许多人的共同参与。

Every step takes time, patience and the support of many people.

01
✚

发现与紧急救助Rescue and urgent care

接回受伤、患病或处于危险中的流浪动物，先完成基础检查和隔离观察。

We bring injured, sick or at-risk animals to safety, then begin basic checks and quarantine observation.

02
♡

治疗与身体恢复Treatment and recovery

配合医疗、营养和清洁照护，让它们慢慢恢复健康，也重新建立安全感。

Medical care, nutrition and a clean environment help them regain their health and sense of safety.

03
♥

长期照护与陪伴Long-term care and companionship

为暂时无法领养的孩子提供稳定居所、日常活动和持续的个体关注。

Animals not yet ready for adoption receive a stable home, daily activity and individual attention.

04
⌂

寻找合适的家Finding the right home

了解领养家庭的生活环境与期待，认真匹配，并持续关注领养后的适应情况。

We learn about each adopter's home and expectations, make careful matches and follow their adjustment.
